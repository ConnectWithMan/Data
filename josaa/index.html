About JoSAA cutoffs

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) conducts centralised counselling for admission to IITs, NITs, IIITs and other Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs). Ranks for IITs are JEE Advanced ranks, while ranks for NITs, IIITs and GFTIs are JEE Main ranks. Looking for special rounds after JoSAA? Browse CSAB cutoff data.
