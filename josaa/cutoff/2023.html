Lower rank is better. “Closing shift” is the Round 6 closing rank minus the Round 1 closing rank: a positive number means the seat closed at a higher rank (easier to get) by Round 6. Seats with a dash have no Round 6 entry. Ranks are JEE Advanced ranks for IITs and JEE Main ranks for NITs, IIITs and GFTIs; for reserved categories they are category ranks.
