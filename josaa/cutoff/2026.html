Rank methodology & data notes

JoSAA uses JEE (Advanced) ranks for IITs (and IISc Bangalore) and JEE (Main) ranks for NITs, IIITs, and other GFTIs. OPEN seats use Common Rank List (CRL) ranks, while reserved categories (EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and PwD) use category ranks.

Lower rank indicates higher merit. “Closing shift” is the compared target round closing rank minus the starting round closing rank: a positive number means the seat closed at a higher rank (easier to get) in the later round. Official source: https://josaa.nic.in/.
