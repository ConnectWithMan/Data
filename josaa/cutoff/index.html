About JoSAA cutoffs

Each JoSAA cutoff table lists opening and closing ranks across institutes, programs, quotas, seat categories and gender pools. Ranks for IITs are JEE Advanced ranks, while ranks for NITs, IIITs and GFTIs are JEE Main ranks. Also see CSAB special round cutoffs.
