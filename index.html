Official Rank Methodology & Data Sources

JoSAA Counselling (josaa.nic.in): Uses JEE (Advanced) ranks for IITs (and IISc Bangalore) and JEE (Main) ranks for NITs, IIITs and other GFTIs. OPEN seats use CRL ranks, while reserved categories use category-wise ranks.

CSAB Special Counselling (csab.nic.in): Uses All India JEE (Main) CRL (Common Rank List) ranks for all categories across NITs, IIITs and other GFTIs. IITs do not participate in CSAB Special rounds.
