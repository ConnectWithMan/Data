Rank methodology & data notes

CSAB Special rounds use All India CRL (Common Rank List) ranks of JEE (Main) for all categories across NITs, IIITs, and other GFTIs. IITs do not participate in CSAB Special counselling.

Lower rank indicates higher merit. “Closing shift” is the compared target round closing rank minus the starting round closing rank: a positive number means the seat closed at a higher rank (easier to get) in the later round. Official source: https://csab.nic.in/.
