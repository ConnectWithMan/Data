Seat Round 1 Round 2

Lower rank is better. “Closing shift” is the Round 2 closing rank minus the Round 1 closing rank: a positive number means the seat closed at a higher rank (easier to get) in Round 2. A dash means the seat has no entry in that round, and the closing shift is shown only when a seat has both. Ranks are JEE Main ranks; for reserved categories they are category ranks. This data has no IITs. Institute type is worked out from the institute name: NITs and IIITs are picked out by name and every other institute is listed as an other GFTI.

Data is compiled from official counselling releases and may contain errors. Check the official JoSAA and CSAB websites before making decisions.
