About CSAB cutoffs

CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) runs the follow-up special rounds for seats that remain vacant after JoSAA counselling across NITs, IIITs and other GFTIs (IITs do not participate in CSAB). Ranks are All India JEE Main CRL ranks. Looking for main counselling rounds? Browse JoSAA cutoff data.
